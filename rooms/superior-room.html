Superior Room

Our largest room — more space to relax, work, and spread out

The Superior Room is the most spacious accommodation at M2 Rooms & Stays — a generously proportioned retreat with a king bed, dedicated work desk, and room to genuinely unwind. Whether you're in Patong for a longer stay, travelling for business, or simply want more space to relax after a day at the beach, the Superior Room delivers. Newly renovated with quality furnishings throughout, it offers boutique-hotel comfort at a guesthouse rate. Free high-speed WiFi makes it equally suitable for remote workers. Patong Beach is a 5-minute walk away.
