Frequently Asked Questions

Is there free WiFi?
Yes — free WiFi is included in all rooms.
Can I smoke in the rooms?
Smoking is not permitted inside any rooms. The Big Room with Porch has direct access to a shared outdoor porch where smoking is allowed.
Is early check-in available?
We'll do our best. WhatsApp us in advance and we'll let you know availability. Early check-in is subject to room readiness.
How far is the beach?
Patong Beach is about a 5 minute walk from M2.
Is parking available?
WhatsApp us to discuss parking options — availability depends on your dates.
Do you accept long-stay guests?
Yes — we welcome longer stays. Contact us via WhatsApp to discuss rates for weekly or monthly bookings.
