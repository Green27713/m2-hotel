Getting Here

From Phuket International Airport to M2 Rooms & Stays — your options.

Airport Bus (Smart Bus) ฿100 per person

The most affordable option. The Smart Bus runs from the airport to Patong Beach regularly. Journey takes around 60–90 minutes depending on traffic. Get off at the Patong Beach stop and it's a short walk or tuk-tuk from there.

Shared Minivan ฿200–300 per person

Shared minivans operate from the airport to Patong. Faster than the bus and reasonably priced. Available from the arrivals hall — look for licensed operators.

Private Taxi ฿800–1,200

Private metered or fixed-rate taxis are available from the official taxi desk in arrivals. Journey takes 45–60 minutes. Book through the official desk to avoid being overcharged.

M2 Airport Transfer Contact us for rates

We can arrange a private transfer directly to our door. Contact us with your flight details before arrival and we'll organise everything. The most stress-free option after a long flight.

Getting Around Patong ฿100–300 per ride

Tuk-tuks and motorbike taxis are the standard way to get around Patong. Agree the price before you get in. Scooter rentals are widely available from around ฿200–300/day for exploring further afield.
