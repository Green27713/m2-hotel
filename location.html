Getting Here

How to Reach Us

✿

✈️ From Phuket Airport

Take a metered taxi from the airport to Patong Beach — approximately 45 minutes, expect to pay around ฿600–800 depending on traffic. Tell the driver "Patong Beach" and share our exact address via WhatsApp before you land. Private transfers can also be arranged.

🚖 Taxi / Grab

Grab (the Thai equivalent of Uber) works well in Phuket and is the easiest way to get around without negotiating prices. Regular metered taxis are also plentiful in Patong. Both drop you right at the door.

🛺 Tuk-Tuk

Tuk-tuks are everywhere in Patong and are fine for short distances within the area. Always agree on the price before you get in. For longer distances from other parts of Phuket, taxi or Grab is more reliable.

🚌 Local Songthaew

Red songthaew (shared trucks) run along the main roads in Patong for a fixed low price. Good for getting to Central Festival mall or other nearby areas on the cheap. Not recommended for airport transfers.

🛵 Scooter Rental

Several scooter rental shops are a short walk from M2. Renting a scooter gives you full freedom to explore Phuket at your own pace. International driving licence required. WhatsApp us and we can point you to the nearest reliable rental.

💬 Need Help?

WhatsApp us before you arrive and we'll send you the exact location pin. We're happy to advise on the best way to get here from wherever you're coming from — airport, ferry pier, or anywhere else on the island.
