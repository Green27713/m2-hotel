House Rules

Simple, fair rules so everyone has a great stay

M2 is a small guesthouse and we ask all guests to respect these straightforward policies. They exist to ensure a comfortable stay for everyone. If you have questions, WhatsApp us before booking.

🕐 Check-in & Check-out

  • Check-in from 2:00 PM
  • Check-out by 12:00 PM (noon)
  • Early check-in subject to availability — WhatsApp us in advance
  • Late check-out by arrangement — ask when you arrive
  • Please inform us of late arrivals in advance

🚬 Smoking Policy

  • Smoking is strictly prohibited inside all rooms
  • Smoking is only permitted on the shared outdoor porch
  • Only the Big Room with Porch has direct porch access
  • Other guests may access the porch area
  • Please dispose of cigarette ends responsibly

🔇 Noise & Quiet Hours

  • Quiet hours: 11:00 PM to 8:00 AM
  • Please be considerate of other guests at all times
  • No loud music or gatherings in rooms
  • Keep voices low in corridors and shared areas

👥 Guests & Visitors

  • Only registered guests may stay in rooms
  • Visitors must be agreed in advance
  • Thai law requires passport/ID registration for all guests
  • Please provide ID at check-in
  • Unregistered overnight guests are not permitted

🧹 Room Care

  • Please treat the room and furnishings with care
  • Report any damage or maintenance issues promptly
  • Do not move furniture or alter the room setup
  • Housekeeping is available — ask at check-in for schedule
  • Guests are responsible for any damage caused

💳 Payments & Cancellation

  • Booking and payment terms as per Booking.com listing
  • Cancellation policy is shown at time of booking
  • Please read cancellation terms before confirming
  • Direct bookings — WhatsApp us for terms

🔑 Keys & Security

  • Please return room key at check-out
  • Lost keys may incur a replacement charge
  • Lock your room when you leave
  • M2 is not responsible for valuables left unsecured

🌿 General

  • No illegal substances on the premises
  • No weapons of any kind permitted
  • Management reserves the right to refuse service
  • Guests causing disturbance may be asked to leave
  • Be kind — we're a small family-run guesthouse

Questions About Our Policies?

We're happy to clarify anything before you book. WhatsApp us directly.
