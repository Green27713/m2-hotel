The Best Local Thai Food Near Patong Beach

📅 2 May 2025 ⏱ 4 min read ✍️ M2 Rooms & Stays

Skip the tourist menus. These are the restaurants, stalls, and markets where locals actually eat — all within walking distance of M2.

← Back to Blog

Patong has no shortage of places to eat, but finding genuinely good Thai food amid the tourist restaurants takes a little local knowledge. Menus printed in six languages and photos of every dish are usually a warning sign. Here's where our team actually eats.

Banzaan Fresh Market — 5 Min Walk

The best fresh market in Patong, and one of the most underrated spots in all of Phuket. The ground floor is fresh seafood and produce where local restaurants buy their ingredients. The upstairs food court serves some of the most authentic Thai dishes in the area at prices that seem almost impossible — a plate of pad see ew runs around ฿60, a bowl of tom kha around ฿80. Go hungry.

Sabai Corner

A local favourite that's been running for over 20 years. No air conditioning, plastic chairs, overhead fans — and some of the best massaman curry and fresh spring rolls you'll find in Patong. The crowd is almost entirely Thai, which tells you everything. Very large portions, very fair prices.

The Night Market on Rat-U-Thit Road

Every evening from around 5pm, the night market on Rat-U-Thit Road fills with vendors selling grilled meat skewers, papaya salad, mango sticky rice, and fresh coconut. A proper feast for under ฿200. Walk the full length before buying — the quality varies and the best stalls are worth finding.

The Noodle House (Near Sai Nam Yen Road)

Look for the small noodle shop opposite the 7-Eleven on Sai Nam Yen Road. The bowl of boat noodles here — rich, dark, intensely flavoured — is one of the best things you'll eat in Patong. It opens at 8am and sells out by noon. Get there early.

Our Tip: The restaurants with tables right on the beach sand charge 3–4 times the price for average food. Walk one street back from the beachfront and the quality goes up while the price drops immediately. The view is the product, not the cooking.

What to Order

If you're new to Thai food and unsure where to start, these are the dishes worth seeking out in Patong: pad krapow moo (basil pork with egg), khao man gai (poached chicken rice), boat noodles, som tum (green papaya salad), and khao soi — a Northern Thai curry noodle soup that you'll find on menus across Phuket. All are under ฿120 at a proper local spot.
