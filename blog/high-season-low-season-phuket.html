High Season vs Low Season in Phuket — When to Visit

📅 18 April 2025 ⏱ 6 min read ✍️ M2 Rooms & Stays

Both seasons have their magic. Here's an honest breakdown of what to expect, when to find the best deals, and our personal recommendation for when to visit Patong.

← Back to Blog

One of the most common questions we get at M2 is: "When is the best time to visit Phuket?" The honest answer is that it depends entirely on what you're looking for — and both seasons have genuine advantages.

November – April

High Season

Dry, sunny, warm. Calm seas, full beaches, buzzing nightlife. Higher prices, book ahead.

May & October

Shoulder Season

Fewer crowds, 20–40% lower rates, weather mostly good with some afternoon rain.

June – September

Low Season

Monsoon rains, rough seas. Lowest prices, lush greenery, most authentic experience.

High Season — November to April

This is peak Phuket. The weather is consistently dry, sunny, and warm at around 32°C. The Andaman Sea is calm and crystal-clear — ideal for swimming, snorkelling, and island day trips to Phi Phi and the Similan Islands. Patong Beach is at its most vibrant: full sunbeds, busy beach bars, and the full energy of Thai tourism in full swing.

The trade-off is price and availability. Rooms at M2 and across Patong cost more during high season, particularly in December and January when demand peaks. Book as far in advance as possible for this period. The busy period between Christmas and New Year sees prices at their highest across the island.

Shoulder Season — May and October

Our personal favourite time to visit. The crowds thin out noticeably, prices drop by 20–40% from peak rates, and the weather is still largely excellent. May sees some afternoon rain that typically clears quickly, leaving mornings and evenings warm and beautiful. October follows the same pattern as the island transitions back toward high season.

You get essentially the same Patong experience as high season — beach, nightlife, restaurants, day trips — at significantly lower cost and with more room to breathe.

Low Season — June to September

Monsoon season. Rain is frequent and can be heavy, and the sea is rough enough that swimming is often inadvisable. Many beach businesses reduce their hours, and some smaller operators close entirely. However, the island is lush, green, and genuinely beautiful in a different way. Rates are at their lowest, crowds are minimal, and you'll have a far more authentic experience of local Phuket life.

If you don't mind unpredictable weather and are travelling on a tight budget, June to August offers incredible value. You'll find rooms significantly cheaper than high season, and the island still has plenty to offer — temples, markets, inland attractions, and excellent food are unaffected by rain.

Our Recommendation

For guaranteed sunshine and perfect beach swimming: November to February is ideal — just expect to pay full rates and book early. For the best balance of great weather, lower prices, and fewer crowds: April or October hit a genuine sweet spot. On a tight budget and don't mind some rain: June to August delivers extraordinary value for the experience.

Whatever time you visit, Patong has something special to offer. And wherever the forecast lands, we'll make sure your stay at M2 is everything it should be.
