Check-in & Your Stay

Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 12:00 PM (noon).

Early check-in or late check-out can be requested and we'll do our best to accommodate, subject to room availability. Please WhatsApp us in advance if you need either.

Yes — our reception team is on call 24 hours a day. If you arrive late or need assistance outside regular hours, simply call or WhatsApp the number provided at check-in and someone will be with you promptly.

All rooms include air conditioning, flat-screen TV, refrigerator, hot water shower, wardrobe, free high-speed WiFi, and fresh towels. Rooms with balconies have outdoor seating.

The Superior Room also includes a dedicated work desk — ideal for longer stays or remote working.

Yes, housekeeping is available. Please ask at reception at check-in for the schedule and to arrange preferred times.

Location & Getting Here

Patong Beach is approximately a 5–7 minute walk from M2 Rooms & Stays. Close enough to pop back to the room between swims or to drop off shopping.

Yes — we can arrange a private taxi or minivan from Phuket International Airport directly to our door. A fee applies. Please contact us with your flight details before arrival and we'll organise everything.

Alternatively, the airport Smart Bus to Patong costs around ฿100 per person and is a good budget option.

Tuk-tuks and motorbike taxis are the standard way to get around Patong. Always agree on the price before getting in — short rides are typically ฿100–200.

Scooter rentals are available from around ฿200–300 per day and are the most flexible way to explore further afield. Ask us at reception for a trusted rental recommendation.

There is a 7-Eleven approximately 2 minutes walk from the property — handy for snacks, drinks, SIM cards, and ATM access. A local pharmacy is around 3 minutes walk and stocks medicines, sunscreen, and travel essentials.

Banzaan Fresh Market (5 min walk) is the best spot for fresh food and authentic local Thai meals at very reasonable prices.

WiFi & Facilities

Yes — we provide free high-speed internet throughout the property, suitable for video calls and remote work. The lobby lounge is a popular spot for guests who need a comfortable place to work during the day.

Please contact us directly about parking availability. Most guests visiting Patong find that tuk-tuks and scooter rentals are more practical than a private car given the local traffic and parking conditions.

Booking & Payments

When you book directly with us you get our guaranteed best rate — OTA platforms like Booking.com charge commission which can affect pricing. You also get direct contact with us for any requests, questions, or changes, rather than going through a third-party platform.

For bookings made through Booking.com, the cancellation policy is shown at the time of booking — please read it carefully before confirming.

For direct bookings, WhatsApp us and we'll discuss the terms with you. We aim to be as flexible as possible.

Yes — we welcome longer stays and can accommodate small groups across multiple rooms. Please contact us directly via WhatsApp or the enquiry form for group bookings or stays longer than 2 weeks, as we may be able to offer better rates.
